## Differences between the documents

- The biography lists 23 practice areas, including tort and civil-rights matters (for example
  New York Labor Law Claims, Product Liability, False Arrest, Malicious Prosecution). The
  practice-areas document describes five. The site shows the five on the Practice Areas page
  and the full list of 23 on the About page under "Mazzy's practice areas".
- The disclaimers refer to client testimonials and past case outcomes. No testimonials or case
  results were supplied, so none appear on the site.
